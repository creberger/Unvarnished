## Description (max 4000)

A painting, five details, three statements each. Only one is true.

Unvarnished zooms into a part of a masterpiece and asks which statement about it is true. After every pick it marks each statement True or False and explains why, so even a wrong guess teaches you something.

Get all five rounds of a painting right and it's acquired for your collection, where you can look back at it and the five things you learned.

100 PAINTINGS, 700 YEARS
From the 1290s to 1930: Old Masters, Impressionists, Japanese prints, a Chinese handscroll, a Roman-Egyptian mummy portrait, and modern work by Matisse, Kandinsky, Malevich, Klee and Mondrian.

EIGHT COLLECTIONS
Renaissance · Golden Age & Baroque · Rococo & Revolution · Romantics & Realists · Impressionism · After Impressionism · Modern · Asia & Antiquity. Play one, or all of them at once.

ROUNDS THAT GET HARDER
The first two rounds of each painting are warm-ups. By the fifth, every statement sounds right and only a name, a date or a number gives the false ones away.

NO ACCOUNTS, NO ADS, NO TRACKING
Your progress stays on your iPhone. Unvarnished collects no data and works offline.

Every image is in the public domain, from the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Cleveland Museum of Art and public-domain scans hosted on Wikimedia Commons.

Answer **"No, we do not collect data from this app."** The code stores progress in UserDefaults on the device only and makes no network calls.

## Age rating questionnaire — points to answer carefully

Some paintings show artistic nudity (e.g. Garden of Earthly Delights, Bacchus and Ariadne) and violence or death (e.g. Third of May, Raft of the Medusa, Spoliarium, Death of Marat). Answer the nudity and violence questions honestly as infrequent/mild, artistic. App Store Connect computes the rating from your answers.

## Review notes (optional, for the App Review team)

No login required. Pick a category on the home screen to start a painting. Getting all five rounds right adds the painting to Your Collection.
