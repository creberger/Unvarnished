# Unvarnished — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: TODO-date_

Unvarnished does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

**What the app stores.** To remember your progress, the app saves which paintings you have played, which you have added to your collection and when, and the category you last chose. This is kept only on your device, using the standard iOS app storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else.

**No network use.** The app does not connect to the internet. All paintings and text are included in the app.

**No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.** The app has no sign-in, contains no analytics or advertising code, and does not track you across apps or websites.

**Deleting your data.** Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

**Children.** Because the app collects no information, it collects none from children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
