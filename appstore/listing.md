## Description (4000)

Look closer at the world's great paintings.

Unvarnished is an art quiz for curious people. Each painting zooms into five details, from a pearl earring to a name hidden on a knife handle. For each detail you get three statements. Only one is true.

Pick wrong and that statement greys out, so you keep looking until you find the truth. Every answer explains why it's true or false, so even a wrong guess teaches you something. The questions get harder as you go: the first are warm-ups, the last are near misses that differ by a single name, date or number.

Get all five right on the first try and the painting is acquired for Your Collection, along with what you learned about it.

- 100 paintings, from Duccio and Van Eyck to Monet, Van Gogh, Klimt, Kandinsky and Mondrian
- Eight categories: Renaissance, Golden Age & Baroque, Rococo & Revolution, Romantics & Realists, Impressionism, After Impressionism, Modern, and Asia & Antiquity
- Hokusai's Great Wave, a Tang dynasty horse, a Roman-Egyptian mummy portrait and more beyond Europe
- Work by Artemisia Gentileschi, Judith Leyster, Clara Peeters, Rachel Ruysch, Berthe Morisot, Mary Cassatt, Rosa Bonheur and Hilma af Klint
- A short story of each painting once you finish it
- No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

Every image is a public-domain reproduction from the Metropolitan Museum of Art, the Cleveland Museum of Art and Wikimedia Commons.

## Age rating

Answer Apple's questionnaire honestly. Relevant content in the paintings:

- **Nudity:** artistic nudes (Bosch, Titian, Gauguin, Klimt and others). Answer "Infrequent" for nudity.
- **Violence:** scenes of death and execution in historical paintings (The Raft of the Medusa, The Third of May, The Death of Marat, Spoliarium). Answer "Infrequent / Mild" for realistic violence.

That will most likely give a 13+ rating.
